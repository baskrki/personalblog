Put pictures for your posts in this folder.

In a post, show one like this:

  <figure><img src="images/my-picture.png" alt="what the picture shows"><figcaption>Figure 1. Caption.</figcaption></figure>

The address is relative to index.html, so it always starts with images/.
Use lowercase names without spaces (my-picture.png) and match the spelling and
capital letters exactly. Wide pictures shrink to fit the page.
